I'm a research and data analyst with a Ph.D. in Industrial Systems Engineering and 10+ years leading applied projects with public-sector, industry, and academic partners across Canada, the United States, South Korea, and Iran. My work combines simulation, optimization, and machine learning to turn large, messy operational datasets into forecasts, models, and dashboards that decision-makers actually use — across healthcare operations, supply chains, and public-sector planning. Recent work includes an AI-enabled decision-support platform for emergency departments that contributed to up to a 40% reduction in wait times, built during my postdoctoral appointment at the University of Winnipeg. I currently teach Project Management, Operations Management, and the Data Analytics Capstone at the University of Winnipeg's School of Business and Administration.

Research Focus

My research develops data-driven decision-support methods for complex operational systems — combining process mining, discrete-event simulation, mathematical optimization, and machine learning to understand and improve how people, resources, and processes flow through an organization. I've applied this toolkit to emergency department layout design and demand forecasting, patient-trajectory modelling, scheduling and workforce optimization, and industrial supply-chain routing and inventory planning.
