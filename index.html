About

I'm an industrial systems engineer working in operations analytics. I build simulation and optimization models, and dashboards that help organizations plan resources, schedules and capacity.

I've applied this work in logistics and distribution, warehousing, public-sector planning and health services, in Canada, the U.S., South Korea and Iran. I also teach operations and project management at the University of Winnipeg.
