Projects

Selected applied and research projects.

Logistics

Fleet and distribution optimization

Parsia Gas, Tehran · consulting · 2017–2022

Designed optimization engines for a fleet-management platform serving an industrial gas network: driver assignment, multi-constraint routing, product–vehicle matching and scheduling. Formulated mixed-integer and stochastic models for distribution, inventory and routing, with heuristics for instances too large to solve exactly.

Outcome: Engines deployed in the platform and validated with A/B tests and benchmarking; management dashboards for routing and inventory.

Tools: Gurobi, CPLEX, Python, Excel

Public services

Forecasting and scheduling platform for a provincial health system

University of Winnipeg (postdoctoral appointment), with Shared Health Manitoba · 2023–2025

Helped build one reusable decision-support framework that combines demand forecasting, outcome prediction, what-if simulation and scheduling optimization. Harmonized data definitions across source systems for 1M+ records and built audit-traceable KPI logic feeding Power BI.

Outcome: ~90% forecast accuracy; validated simulation models projecting up to 40% shorter waits. A shadow implementation is being planned.

Tools: Python, SQL, Power BI, SimPy, Gurobi

Consulting

Simulation-based process redesign

Insight Strategies, LLC · U.S. clients, remote · 2023–2024

Built and validated discrete-event simulation models of current operations from system data, staff surveys and interviews, then used simulation-based optimization to test redesigned workflows and staffing with frontline teams.

Outcome: Redesign scenarios projecting ~30% shorter length of stay; Excel and Power BI dashboards to track results after go-live.

Tools: Arena, SimPy, Python, Power BI

Public services

Multi-site data standard and electronic triage

Isfahan University of Medical Sciences & Farabi Hospital · 2017–2021

Co-led a shared data standard and KPI set across several sites, and the design and rollout of an electronic triage system, owning its analytics: data collection tools, a risk-scoring method, forecasting models and dashboards.

Outcome: Consistent cross-site performance measurement; dashboards adopted by clinical and administrative users.

Tools: Python, SQL, Excel
