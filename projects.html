Projects

Selected applied research and consulting projects, 2012–present.

AI Decision-Support Platform for Emergency Departments

Machine Learning Simulation Power BI

Postdoctoral project at the University of Winnipeg, with Shared Health Manitoba, building a reusable AI-enabled decision-support platform for ED operations: demand forecasting, patient-outcome prediction, an AI "what-if" scenario simulator, and scheduling/workforce optimization.

Up to 40% reduction in wait times · ~90% forecast accuracy · ~2–3 hours saved per physician/week

Emergency Department Simulation-Based Redesign

Discrete-Event Simulation Optimization

Consulting engagement with Insight Strategies, LLC for U.S. hospital clients. Built and validated an as-is discrete-event simulation of ED flow from EDIS data and provider interviews, then used simulation-based optimization to design and test redesign scenarios with providers.

~30% improvement in length of stay · ~35% reduction in left-without-being-seen

COVID-19 E-Triage & Multi-ED Data Harmonization

Data Standards Risk Scoring

Co-led a multi-hospital initiative with Isfahan University of Medical Sciences and Farabi Hospital to harmonize COVID-19 ED data collection across sites and design/roll out an electronic triage system, including the risk-scoring methodology behind it.

Harmonized data standard adopted across multiple EDs · quantified impact of E-triage on patient surge

Industrial Gas Network Optimization

Mixed-Integer Programming Gurobi

Project-based consulting for Parsia Gas (Tehran). Formulated MILP models for distribution, inventory, and vehicle routing across an industrial gas network, with custom heuristics for routing instances too large for exact solution methods.

Management-facing decision dashboards for distribution & inventory planning

Process-Mining-Based ED Layout Optimization

Process Mining Facility Layout

Ph.D. research at Yonsei University's System Optimization Laboratory, with Severance Hospital. Built an ED event log from raw clinical data, applied process mining to discover process models and bottlenecks, and developed simulation/optimization models for facility-layout design.

Published in HERD Journal (2017, cited 77) and Value in Health (2023, cited 19)

Applied Analytics Capstone Program

Teaching Power BI

Designed and coordinate the University of Winnipeg's graduate Capstone and Project Management courses, supervising student teams that deliver Power BI dashboards and decision briefs for public-sector clients, including the City of Winnipeg, using Statistics Canada, CMHC, and municipal open data.

100% student/course satisfaction across courses taught to date
